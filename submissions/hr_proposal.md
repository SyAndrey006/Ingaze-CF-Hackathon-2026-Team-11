## Key Problems

### Root-cause hypotheses

- Unclear role information may weaken candidate attraction (Engineering)
- Manual workflows may limit reporting coverage and consume staff time
- Expectation drift may contribute to early exits (Berlin & Milan — Engineering)
- A management practice may contribute to exits (Berlin & Milan — Engineering)

- A delayed hire process may contribute to agency dependence

### Reported signals

- Expectation Gap, Manager Support, Career Clarity, Documented Broken Promise (Berlin & Milan — Engineering): Glassdoor/Indeed rating dropped to 3.2/5 in the Milan and Berlin product hubs (vs. 4.3/5 in the Amsterdam core infrastructure hub), with a 1.3-star gap between external recruiting claims ('cloud autonomy') and recent engineering reviews citing 'rigid project micromanagement and unclear promotion criteria'.
- Application Loss (Engineering): Inbound qualified senior engineering applications are down 38% YoY
- Generic Ads, Role Info Gap Verified (Engineering): job descriptions use generic corporate boilerplate ('dynamic fast-paced team player') with zero mention of distributed systems architecture, tech stack autonomy, or engineering leveling.

- Manager Support, Career Clarity, Burnout: Internal pulse notes estimate €1.8M in annual financial turnover risk driven by Weak Middle Management (€700k), Career Path Opacity (€450k), and Workload Burnout (€300k).
- Employer Reputation Weak

## What NOT to Do / Strengths to Protect

### Guardrails

- Defer external advocacy until target-cohort readiness, participation and trust are verified.
- Exclude retail store shift rotation for this non-retail workforce.
- Preserve documented strengths. Measure pilot outcomes in the affected group before widening rollout.
- Defer salary changes pending verified compensation problems in comparable roles.

### Deferred requests

- **External Staffing Dependency:** More scoped evidence is required before proposing this intervention.

### Strengths to protect

- Base compensation and equity packages are benchmarked at the 82nd percentile for European FinTech (0% offer rejections due to base pay), and the Amsterdam Core Platform / SRE division (320 engineers) maintains 94% annual retention with high technical pride.

### Cohort and site differences

- 16% of newly hired Senior Software Engineers and PMs in the Milan and Berlin product squads leave within their first 6 months.
